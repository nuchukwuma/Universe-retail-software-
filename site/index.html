Point of sale for small shops

Sell, stock and see your profit from one desk.

HordeDesk is a till and back office for supermarkets, pharmacies, boutiques and beauty shops. It runs on any Windows laptop, works without internet, and keeps every sale on your own computer.

Try it in your browser Download for Windows

Free. Windows 8.1, 10 and 11. No account needed.

Everything a shop counter needs

A till anyone can use

Tap product tiles, scan a barcode or type part of a name. Hold a bill for one customer while you serve the next.

Stock that stays right

Every sale takes stock off the shelf. Low-stock lists, stock counts and deliveries straight from the scanner.

Suppliers and purchase orders

Order from your suppliers, receive deliveries in full or in part, and keep cost prices up to date as you go.

Profit, not just sales

Daily, weekly and best-seller reports with cost of goods and profit. Export any report to Excel.

The right access for each person

Owner, manager and cashier accounts. Cashiers never see cost prices, and large discounts need a manager.

Works without internet

No monthly fee and no cloud. Your data stays on the laptop, with an automatic daily backup to a USB drive.
