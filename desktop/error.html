Universe POS could not start

The app could not open the shop's database.

The app keeps the shop's data in its own database on this laptop. Try this:

  1. Wait one minute and press Try again. After a power cut the database checks its files first, which can take a moment.
  2. Still not working? Restart the laptop and open Universe POS again.
  3. If MongoDB was installed separately (older set-ups): press the Windows key, type services.msc and press Enter. Find MongoDB Server, right-click it and choose Start.
  4. Otherwise press Open log folder and send universe-pos.log and mongod.log to whoever looks after the app.
